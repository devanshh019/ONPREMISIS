API STANDARD 570: PIPING INSPECTION CODE
In-service Inspection, Rating, Repair, and Alteration of Piping Systems

Section 5.4: Circuit Thickness Measurements and Injection Point Inspection
Process piping circuits in hydrocarbon service shall have designated thickness measurement locations (TMLs).

Key Directives:
- Injection points, control valve stations, and turbulent mixing tees require 100% UT scanning.
- Deadlegs and bypass piping around control valves (e.g., bypass loops around FV valves) must be examined for stagnant acid condensation and localized corrosion.
- Minimum remaining thickness must account for internal design pressure and structural minimum wall thickness per ASME B31.3.
