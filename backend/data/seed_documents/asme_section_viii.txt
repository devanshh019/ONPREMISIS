=============================================================================
ASME BOILER & PRESSURE VESSEL CODE (BPVC) SECTION VIII, DIVISION 1
Design of Cylindrical Shells, Heads, and Material Allowable Stress Standards
=============================================================================

1. SCOPE AND DESIGN OF CYLINDRICAL SHELLS UNDER INTERNAL PRESSURE (UG-27)
For cylindrical shells subjected to internal design pressure (P), the minimum required thickness (t_min) exclusive of corrosion allowance is calculated as:

Circumferential Stress (Longitudinal Joints):
    t_min = (P * R) / (S * E - 0.6 * P)

Where:
    P = Internal design pressure (MPa or psi)
    R = Internal radius of shell course before corrosion allowance is added (mm or inches)
    S = Maximum allowable stress value of material at design temperature (MPa or psi)
    E = Joint efficiency factor for welded joints (dimensionless, per Table UW-12)
        - E = 1.00 for Type 1 butt joints with 100% full radiography (RT-1)
        - E = 0.85 for Type 1 butt joints with spot radiography (RT-2/RT-3)
        - E = 0.70 for Type 1 butt joints with no radiography (visual examination only)

Longitudinal Stress (Circumferential Joints):
    t_min = (P * R) / (2 * S * E + 0.4 * P)

2. FORMED HEAD DESIGN FORMULAS (UG-32)
- Ellipsoidal Heads (2:1 Standard): t_min = (P * D) / (2 * S * E - 0.2 * P)
  Where D = Inside diameter of head skirt (mm).
- Torispherical Heads (ASME Flanged and Dished): t_min = (0.885 * P * L) / (S * E - 0.1 * P)
  Where L = Inside spherical crown radius (mm).
- Hemispherical Heads: t_min = (P * R) / (2 * S * E - 0.2 * P)

3. MATERIAL ALLOWABLE STRESS TABLES (ASME SECTION II PART D)
- SA-516 Grade 70 Carbon Steel:
  * Design Temperature -29°C to 260°C: S = 138 MPa (20,000 psi)
  * Design Temperature 350°C: S = 120 MPa (17,400 psi)
  * Design Temperature 400°C: S = 92 MPa (13,300 psi)
- SA-387 Grade 11 Class 2 (1.25Cr-0.5Mo Alloy Steel):
  * Design Temperature up to 450°C: S = 142 MPa (20,600 psi)
- SA-240 Type 304 Stainless Steel:
  * Design Temperature up to 300°C: S = 115 MPa (16,700 psi)
- SA-240 Type 316L Stainless Steel:
  * Design Temperature up to 300°C: S = 108 MPa (15,600 psi)

4. HYDROSTATIC PROOF TEST REQUIREMENTS (UG-99)
Completed pressure vessels shall pass a hydrostatic test at a pressure not less than:
    P_test = 1.3 * MAWP * (S_test_temp / S_design_temp)
Where MAWP is the Maximum Allowable Working Pressure.
