CHAPTER 1: ASME BOILER & PRESSURE VESSEL CODE (BPVC) SECTION VIII, DIVISION 1
=============================================================================

1.1 SCOPE AND JURISDICTION
ASME BPVC Section VIII Division 1 provides rules and mandatory requirements covering the design, fabrication, inspection, testing, and certification of pressure vessels operating at internal or external pressures exceeding 15 psig (103 kPa).

1.2 DESIGN OF CYLINDRICAL SHELLS UNDER INTERNAL PRESSURE (UG-27)
For cylindrical shells subjected to internal design pressure (P), the minimum required thickness (t_min) exclusive of corrosion allowance is calculated as follows:

1.2.1 Circumferential Stress (Longitudinal Joints):
When the thickness does not exceed one-half of the inside radius, or P does not exceed 0.385 * S * E:
    t_min = (P * R) / (S * E - 0.6 * P)

Where:
    P = Internal design pressure (MPa or psi)
    R = Internal radius of shell course before corrosion allowance is added (mm or inches)
    S = Maximum allowable stress value of material at design temperature (MPa or psi)
    E = Joint efficiency factor for welded joints (dimensionless, per Table UW-12)
        - E = 1.00 for Type 1 butt joints with 100% full radiography (RT-1)
        - E = 0.85 for Type 1 butt joints with spot radiography (RT-2/RT-3)
        - E = 0.70 for Type 1 butt joints with no radiography (visual examination only)

1.2.2 Longitudinal Stress (Circumferential Joints):
When the thickness does not exceed one-half of the inside radius, or P does not exceed 1.25 * S * E:
    t_min = (P * R) / (2 * S * E + 0.4 * P)

1.3 FORMED HEAD DESIGN FORMULAS (UG-32)
1.3.1 Ellipsoidal Heads (2:1 Standard Semi-Ellipsoidal):
    t_min = (P * D) / (2 * S * E - 0.2 * P)
Where D = Inside diameter of head skirt (mm).

1.3.2 Torispherical Heads (ASME Flanged and Dished):
    t_min = (0.885 * P * L) / (S * E - 0.1 * P)
Where L = Inside spherical crown radius (mm).

1.3.3 Hemispherical Heads:
When thickness does not exceed 0.356 * R:
    t_min = (P * R) / (2 * S * E - 0.2 * P)

1.4 MATERIAL ALLOWABLE STRESS TABLES (ASME SECTION II PART D)
- SA-516 Grade 70 Carbon Steel:
  * Design Temperature -29°C to 260°C: S = 138 MPa (20,000 psi)
  * Design Temperature 350°C: S = 120 MPa (17,400 psi)
  * Design Temperature 400°C: S = 92 MPa (13,300 psi)
- SA-387 Grade 11 Class 2 (1.25Cr-0.5Mo Alloy Steel):
  * Design Temperature up to 450°C: S = 142 MPa (20,600 psi)
- SA-240 Type 304 Stainless Steel:
  * Design Temperature up to 300°C: S = 115 MPa (16,700 psi)
- SA-240 Type 316L Stainless Steel:
  * Design Temperature up to 300°C: S = 108 MPa (15,600 psi)

1.5 HYDROSTATIC PROOF TEST REQUIREMENTS (UG-99)
Completed pressure vessels shall pass a hydrostatic test at a pressure not less than:
    P_test = 1.3 * MAWP * (S_test_temp / S_design_temp)
Where MAWP is the Maximum Allowable Working Pressure stamped on the nameplate.

CHAPTER 2: API 510 IN-SERVICE PRESSURE VESSEL INSPECTION CODE
=============================================================================

2.1 SCOPE
API 510 covers in-service inspection, repair, alteration, and rerating activities for pressure vessels and pressure-relieving devices used in petroleum refineries and chemical processing facilities.

2.2 CORROSION RATE DETERMINATION
2.2.1 Long-Term Corrosion Rate (LT):
    C_r(LT) = (t_initial - t_actual) / (Time between t_initial and t_actual in years)

2.2.2 Short-Term Corrosion Rate (ST):
    C_r(ST) = (t_previous - t_actual) / (Time between t_previous and t_actual in years)

2.3 REMAINING SAFE OPERATING LIFE (RL)
The remaining safe operating life of a pressure vessel course or nozzle is determined by:
    Remaining Life (years) = (t_actual - t_required) / Corrosion Rate (mm/year)

Where:
    t_actual = Smallest recorded thickness measurement from ultrasonic testing (UT)
    t_required = Minimum required thickness calculated per ASME BPVC Sec VIII UG-27
    Corrosion Rate = Governing corrosion rate (higher of ST or LT unless justified)

2.4 MAXIMUM INSPECTION INTERVALS (API 510 SECTION 6)
2.4.1 Internal or On-Stream Inspection:
The period between internal inspections or on-stream ultrasonic thickness evaluations shall not exceed:
    Interval_max = Minimum( 10 years, Remaining Life / 2 )
If the remaining life is less than 4 years, the inspection interval shall be determined by the vessel engineer but shall not exceed 2 years.

2.5 TEMPORARY REPAIRS & WELD OVERLAY CLADDING
- Fillet-welded patches shall not be used on pressure vessels unless approved by the pressure vessel engineer and authorized inspector.
- Corrosion-resistant weld overlay (Inconel 625 or 309L/316L SS) requires a minimum deposit thickness of 3.0 mm and 100% liquid penetrant inspection (PT) of the final pass.

CHAPTER 3: API 570 PROCESS PIPING INSPECTION CODE
=============================================================================

3.1 SCOPE AND PIPE RETIREMENT THICKNESS
API 570 governs inspection, rating, repair, and alteration of in-service metallic and fiberglass process piping systems.

3.2 MINIMUM THICKNESS FORMULA (ASME B31.3 PROCESS PIPING)
    t_min = (P * D) / (2 * (S * E + P * Y))

Where:
    P = Internal design pressure (MPa or psig)
    D = Outside diameter of pipe (mm or inches)
    S = Allowable stress per ASME B31.3 Table A-1 (MPa)
    E = Longitudinal quality factor / joint efficiency (0.85 to 1.0)
    Y = Coefficient per ASME B31.3 (Y = 0.4 for ferritic steels below 482°C)

3.3 PIPING CLASS INSPECTION FREQUENCIES
- Class 1 (High Hazard: Flammable gases, H2S > 3%, anhydrous ammonia):
  * Visual external inspection: 5 years
  * Ultrasonic thickness measurement (UT CML): 5 years
- Class 2 (Moderate Hazard: Hydrocarbons, steam, hot water, sour water):
  * Visual external inspection: 5 years
  * Thickness measurement: 10 years
- Class 3 (Low Hazard: Nitrogen, lube oil, non-flammable cooling water):
  * Visual external inspection: 10 years
  * Thickness measurement: Optional / 10 years

CHAPTER 4: THERMAL & HEAT EXCHANGER DESIGN (TEMA & IS-4503)
=============================================================================

4.1 HEAT TRANSFER FUNDAMENTALS
Heat duty (Q) transferred across a heat exchanger is governed by:
    Q = m_dot * Cp * (T_in - T_out)  [for single phase fluid without phase change]
    Q = U * A * LMTD * F             [overall heat exchanger equation]

Where:
    m_dot = Mass flow rate (kg/s)
    Cp = Specific heat capacity (kJ/kg·K)
    U = Overall heat transfer coefficient (W/m²·K)
    A = Total heat transfer area (m²)
    F = LMTD correction factor (F = 1.0 for pure counter-current flow; F < 1.0 for multi-pass shell and tube)

4.2 LOG MEAN TEMPERATURE DIFFERENCE (LMTD)
For a counter-current flow heat exchanger:
    Delta_T1 = T_hot_in - T_cold_out
    Delta_T2 = T_hot_out - T_cold_in
    LMTD = (Delta_T1 - Delta_T2) / ln( Delta_T1 / Delta_T2 )

4.3 TYPICAL OVERALL HEAT TRANSFER COEFFICIENTS (U):
- Water to Water: 800 - 1500 W/m²·K
- Steam to Heavy Fuel Oil: 150 - 300 W/m²·K
- Hydrocarbon Vapor to Water Condenser: 300 - 600 W/m²·K
- Light Gas to Gas: 20 - 80 W/m²·K

CHAPTER 5: PUBLIC PROCUREMENT & GOVERNANCE RULES (GFR 2017)
=============================================================================

5.1 GENERAL PRINCIPLES OF PUBLIC BUYING (RULE 144)
Every authority delegated the financial powers of procuring goods in public interest shall ensure:
1. Specifications are clear, generic, and do not favor any single proprietary firm.
2. The procurement process is competitive, transparent, and fair to all qualified vendors.
3. Offers meet techno-commercial specifications before opening financial bids (Two-Bid System).

5.2 TWO-BID SYSTEM (RULE 160 & RULE 163)
For high-value plant equipment and turnkey contracts exceeding INR 25 Lakhs:
- Envelope 1: Technical & Commercial Bid (containing credentials, quality certifications, design proofs, EMD).
- Envelope 2: Financial / Price Bid.
Financial bids of only those bidders who meet the technical qualifying requirements shall be opened.

5.3 EARNEST MONEY DEPOSIT (EMD) & PERFORMANCE SECURITY (RULE 170 & 171)
- EMD (Bid Security): 2% to 5% of estimated tender value.
- Performance Security (PBG): 5% to 10% of contract value, valid for warranty period plus 60 days.

5.4 PURCHASE PREFERENCE (MAKE IN INDIA ORDER 2017)
- Class-I Local Supplier: Local content >= 50% (eligible for purchase preference when L1 quote is non-local).
- Class-II Local Supplier: Local content between 20% and 49%.
- Non-Local Supplier: Local content < 20%.

CHAPTER 6: INDUSTRIAL PLANT SAFETY & P&ID DRAWING INTERLOCKS (OISD / OSHA)
=============================================================================

6.1 DOUBLE BLOCK AND BLEED (DBB) ISOLATION (OISD-STD-118)
Positive isolation via Double Block and Bleed valve arrangements is mandatory on:
1. Hydrocarbon lines operating above 20 bar or 150°C during maintenance shutdowns.
2. Fuel gas lines leading into fired heaters and boilers during burner maintenance.
3. H2S-bearing sour crude feed trains and toxic chemical injection lines.

6.2 PRESSURE RELIEF VALVE (PRV/PSV) DESIGN BASIS (API 520 / 526)
Pressure safety valves shall be sized to relieve the maximum probable overpressure scenario:
- Blocked discharge
- Fire envelope exposure (API 521 wetted surface area heat absorption)
- Tube rupture in shell-and-tube heat exchangers
- External utility power failure
Set pressure shall not exceed 100% MAWP for single relief valve installations, or 105% MAWP for supplemental multiple relief valves.
